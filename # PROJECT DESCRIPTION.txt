# PROJECT DESCRIPTION

## Kaldra Video Intelligence Platform

## 1. Project Overview

Kaldra is a video-link analytics and marketing intelligence platform that allows users to paste links from popular video platforms and generate new branded, trackable links.

Supported video platforms may include:

* YouTube
* TikTok
* Instagram
* Facebook
* Vimeo
* Twitch
* X
* Dailymotion
* Other publicly accessible video URLs

Instead of sharing the original video link directly, users share a Kaldra-generated link such as:

`watch.kaldra.ng/abc123`

When someone clicks the Kaldra link, the platform records permitted analytics information and then redirects the visitor to the original video.

The main purpose of Kaldra is not simply to shorten links. Its real value is to help creators, marketers, churches, businesses, educators, event organizers, influencers, and agencies understand how their video links perform across different platforms, audiences, locations, campaigns, and devices.

Kaldra should transform raw click data into useful marketing intelligence and practical recommendations.

The central product message is:

**Turn every shared video link into actionable marketing intelligence.**

---

# 2. The Problem Kaldra Solves

Millions of people share video links every day through WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, X, email, websites, QR codes, and other channels.

However, when users share the original video link, they often have limited control over the analytics.

For example, a user may know that a YouTube video received 5,000 views, but they may not know:

* How many people arrived from WhatsApp
* Which influencer generated the most traffic
* Which campaign performed best
* Which QR code generated the most clicks
* Which city produced the highest engagement
* What time most people clicked
* Whether visitors were new or returning
* Which device visitors used
* Which link placement performed best
* Which marketing channel should receive more attention

Kaldra solves this problem by placing an intelligent tracking and campaign-management layer between the shared link and the original video destination.

# 3. Main User Flow

The platform should provide a simple and fast experience.

## Step 1: Create an Account

A user visits the Kaldra website and creates an account using:

* Email and password
* Google sign-in
* Apple sign-in
* Microsoft sign-in

Business users may also be able to create an organization or team account.

## Step 2: Paste a Video Link

The user pastes a supported video URL into a clearly visible input field.

Example:

`https://youtube.com/watch?v=example123`

## Step 3: Customize the Link

The user may customize:

* Link title
* Campaign name
* Short-link alias
* Link destination
* Branded domain
* UTM parameters
* Link expiration date
* Password protection
* Geo restrictions
* Tags
* Campaign owner
* Traffic-source label

## Step 4: Generate a Trackable Link

The platform generates a short branded link.

Example:

`watch.kaldra.ng/sundayservice`

## Step 5: Share the Link

The user shares the generated link through:

* WhatsApp
* Facebook
* Instagram
* TikTok
* X
* Email
* SMS
* QR code
* Website
* Printed materials
* Influencers
* Affiliate partners

## Step 6: Track Visitor Activity

When a visitor clicks the link, Kaldra records available and permitted information such as:

* Click date and time
* Device type
* Browser
* Operating system
* Referring source
* Approximate location
* Language
* Campaign parameters
* New or returning visitor status

The visitor is then redirected quickly to the original video.

## Step 7: View Analytics

The link owner can view live analytics from the dashboard.

## Step 8: Receive AI Recommendations

The platform analyzes the data and provides recommendations such as:

* Best time to share future videos
* Best-performing traffic source
* Highest-performing campaign
* Best-performing city or location
* Underperforming channels
* Growth or decline in traffic
* Returning-visitor trends
* Suggested budget allocation
* Suggested campaign improvements

# 12. Analytics and Tracking Requirements

Kaldra should only collect information that is lawful, necessary, disclosed, and permitted.

Possible analytics may include:

* Timestamp
* Referring source
* Device category
* Browser
* Operating system
* Preferred language
* Approximate location
* UTM parameters
* Campaign identifier
* Link identifier
* QR-code identifier
* New or returning visitor status
* Redirect result
* Conversion event, where configured

The platform should avoid promising access to information that cannot reliably be detected.

For example, traffic from some mobile applications may appear as direct or unknown traffic if the application does not send referral information.

Location data based on IP addresses should be described as approximate.

Kaldra should not claim to know a visitor's exact home address or precise physical location unless the visitor has explicitly granted permission for a specific feature.

# 26. Product Differentiation

* Video-focused link intelligence
* Clear campaign attribution
* AI-powered recommendations
* Creator and business use cases
* Influencer-performance tracking
* QR-code intelligence
* Simple reports
* Support for teams and agencies
* Easy-to-understand analytics
* Actionable marketing guidance

The key difference is:

**Bitly helps users create and track short links.**

**Google Analytics helps website owners understand activity on their own websites.**

**Kaldra helps users understand how shared video links perform across social platforms, messaging applications, campaigns, influencers, QR codes, and audiences.**

---

# 27. Final Product Vision

Kaldra should become a complete video-distribution intelligence platform.

A user should be able to paste any supported video link, generate a branded tracking link, share it anywhere, understand who clicked, identify where the traffic came from, compare campaign performance, generate reports, and receive clear AI-powered guidance.

The platform should make advanced analytics understandable even to users who are not marketing experts.

The ultimate vision is to help every creator, organization, church, marketer, educator, and business make better decisions whenever they share video content online.

## Final Brand Statement

**Kaldra turns ordinary video links into intelligent marketing assets.**
